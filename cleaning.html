Built for cleaning teams

From the rota to the timesheet, in one place

Each crew is a roving team in iSite™: a team, not a place. Cleaners sign on wherever the job is, and the office gets an honest record of who was where, and for how long.

Sign on at the door

A cleaner scans their team's QR code on their own phone and picks today's job for that client, or logs an ad-hoc one. No kiosk to install at the client's building, and only your own staff can use the code.

Who is where, right now

A live map shows a pin for each cleaner currently on a job. When a client asks whether someone came in last night, the register shows who signed on, where and for how long.

Clients and the week's rota Field Ops

Keep each client with their address and contact, hold a backlog of jobs, and drag them onto a crew and a day. The planner shows what is scheduled, the planned hours and what is still waiting.

Hours you can pay from Field Ops

Each person's week is built from their own sign-ons, broken down by job and client. A manager approves the week and exports it to CSV for payroll or client billing.

No tracking in between

Location is captured when someone signs on and when they sign off, and at no other time. Your staff get accountability without feeling watched.

Big contracts as fixed sites

For a hospital, campus or shopping centre with a resident team, set it up as a fixed site instead, with its own induction, a geofence and a sign-in point at the staff entrance.

How it works

Set up once, then the week runs itself

  1. 1

    Create your teams

    One roving team per crew or area, each with its own QR code for your cleaners' phones.

  2. 2

    Add clients & jobs

    Record your clients and their regular and one-off jobs.

  3. 3

    Plan the week

    Drag each job onto a crew and a day in the week planner.

  4. 4

    Sign on to the job

    Cleaners scan, pick the job and start. Their time lands against it.

  5. 5

    Approve the week

    Check each person's hours by job and client, approve, and export.
