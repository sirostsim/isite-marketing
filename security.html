Built for security teams

Static sites and mobile officers, in one system

Set up a long-term guarded site as a fixed site with its own boundary, and your mobile officers as a roving team that signs on wherever the job is. Both land in the same register and the same timesheets.

Fixed sites with a boundary

Each guarded site gets a printed QR poster and a geofence. Officers sign on with their phone, and their location is judged against the site boundary: advisory by default, or enforced if you choose.

Mobile officers on the move

Mobile officers scan their team's QR code and pick the job: a lock-up, an unlock or an alarm response. Each sign-on records where it happened, and only your own staff can use the code.

Who is on duty, right now

A live on-site register for every fixed site, and a live map with a pin for each mobile officer on a job. When a client asks who was on last night, the record answers.

Plan the mobile work Field Ops

Keep your clients and their scheduled jobs, then drag each job onto a team and a day. The planner shows what is covered, the planned hours and what is still waiting.

Hours ready for payroll Field Ops

Each officer's week is built from their own sign-ons, by site, job and client. A manager approves the week and exports it to CSV. A shift still open is flagged, not guessed.

Site inductions that stick

Fixed sites can require a versioned site induction before an officer's first shift, approved by a manager with validity dates, so a new officer on a contract has read the site's instructions first.
