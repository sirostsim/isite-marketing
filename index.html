Features

Everything a busy site actually needs

Versioned inductions & approvals

H&S and visitor inductions, versioned and immutable. A manager approves with validity dates, so a historic record shows exactly what each person read and signed.

Four ways through the gate

A shared tablet kiosk, a printed QR / short-code gate poster, a scan on a worker's own phone, or a fast-track carry-over from another of your sites. Never a bypass.

Live attendance & one-tap muster

An always-honest on-site register, backed by append-only events. In an evacuation, managers run an interactive roll-call from any device.

Man-hours you can trust

A shift the worker closed themselves is measured. One auto-closed at the site's maximum is clearly marked estimated, never mixed and always in its own column. CSV export.

Geofencing as evidence

Location is captured at sign-in and sign-out and judged against the site boundary. Advisory by default, so a poor or missing fix never locks a worker out unless you choose to enforce it.

Emergency evacuation SMS

One button texts everyone signed in on the site to evacuate to the assembly point, with a full record of who was reached. Add-on

Access control that holds

Approvals, suspensions, expired cards and lapsed inductions are all enforced by a single engine, and nothing medical or personal ever appears on a gate screen.

Multi-tenant, your branding

Each contractor gets their own colours, sites and data on one platform. Role-based access scopes every screen, so an administrator sees all sites in their own organisation.

Built for compliance

Special-category health data is kept separate and audit-logged on read. One-time invite links, a full audit trail, and data-retention controls come as standard.

How it works

Register once, then it just flows

  1. 1

    Register

    A worker registers on their phone, at the tablet, or from an email invite to your site.

  2. 2

    Induct

    They complete this site's versioned induction, acknowledge the safety briefing and RAMS, and sign.

  3. 3

    Approve

    A manager approves with validity dates, or the worker carries an approved induction across from another of your sites.

  4. 4

    Sign in & out

    At the gate kiosk, by scanning a QR on their phone, or with a short code from a printed poster.

  5. 5

    Report

    Live attendance, man-hours, expiring documents and the muster register all follow automatically.
