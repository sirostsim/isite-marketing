Built for care teams

Visibility for the office, respect for the carer

Each round or area is a roving team in iSite™: a team, not a place. Carers sign on at each visit, and the office has an honest record of who was where, and for how long, without following anyone around.

Sign on at every visit

A carer scans their team's QR code on their own phone and picks the visit from today's list, or logs an extra one. Nothing to install in the client's home, and only your own staff can use the code.

Who is out, and where

A live map shows a pin for each carer currently on a visit, so the office knows who is out and roughly where. The register keeps every sign-on with its time and location.

No tracking in between

Location is captured when a carer signs on and when they sign off, and at no other time. Your carers are accountable for their visits, not tracked through their day.

Plan the week's visits Field Ops

Keep your clients and their regular visits, then drag each visit onto a carer's team and a day. The planner shows what is covered, the planned hours and what still needs someone.

Hours from real visits Field Ops

Each carer's week is built from their own sign-ons, visit by visit. A manager approves the week and exports it to CSV for payroll. A visit still open is flagged, not guessed.

Access that stays in your hands

Role-based access scopes every screen, a suspended account can't sign on, and every action leaves an audit trail. Carers can see their own visit history, and only their own.

iSite™ records your workforce's attendance. It is not a care-planning, medication or care-records system, and holds no information about the people you care for beyond the client name and address you choose to add to a visit.

How it works

Set up once, then the week runs itself

  1. 1

    Create your teams

    One roving team per round or area, each with its own QR code for your carers' phones.

  2. 2

    Add clients & visits

    Record the clients you visit and their regular calls.

  3. 3

    Plan the week

    Drag each visit onto a team and a day in the week planner.

  4. 4

    Sign on at the door

    Carers scan, pick the visit and start. Their time lands against it.

  5. 5

    Approve the week

    Check each carer's hours visit by visit, approve, and export.
