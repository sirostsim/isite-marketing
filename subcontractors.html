Built for subcontractors

Plan it, prove it, get paid for it

Run your crews as roving teams that sign on wherever the job is, and your own small works as fixed sites with inductions and a gate QR. Everything lands in one register and one set of timesheets.

Plan the week's crews Field Ops

Keep your clients and a backlog of jobs, then drag each job onto a crew and a day. The planner shows your crews, what's scheduled, the planned hours and what is still waiting.

Sign on to the job Field Ops

A crew member scans their team's QR code, picks from today's jobs or logs an ad-hoc one, and their time lands against that job. The job moves to in progress on its own.

Timesheets you can stand behind Field Ops

Each person's week is built from their own sign-ons, by job and client. A manager approves it and exports to CSV, ready for payroll and for backing up your applications for payment.

Snags straight to your trade

On sites running iSite™, snags assigned to your trade show on your operatives' own phones to fix, with a before photo and an after photo to prove it's done.

One account, every gate

Operatives keep one iSite™ account, with their CSCS, CPCS, IPAF, PASMA and other cards on it, across every site that uses the platform. No starting again at every gate.

Speak a task AI

On your own sites, say what needs doing and AI writes it up as a task, with the location and the right person matched from your own people. You check it before it's assigned.

How it works

Set up once, then the week runs itself

  1. 1

    Create your crews

    One roving team per crew or division, each with its own QR code.

  2. 2

    Add clients & jobs

    Record the main contractors and clients you work for, and their jobs.

  3. 3

    Plan the week

    Drag each job onto a crew and a day in the week planner.

  4. 4

    Sign on to the job

    Crews scan, pick the job and start. Their time lands against it.

  5. 5

    Approve the week

    Check each person's hours by job and client, approve, and export.
